Model Configuration & Definitions

Target Variable

Churn vs. Retention: The model predicts the probability of a policyholder cancelling or lapsing their policy at the next renewal date.

  • 0 (Retain): Policy renewed.
  • 1 (Churn): Policy cancelled or lapsed.

Observation Window

The model utilizes customer behavioral data, claim history, and policy modifications from the previous 3 years leading up to the renewal decision point.

Prediction Window

The prediction is valid for the upcoming renewal cycle (12 months).
